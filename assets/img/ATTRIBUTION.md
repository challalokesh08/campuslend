## What this means for you

- **CC0 / Public domain** (the Casio, the backpack): no conditions, do as you like.
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA**: you must keep the credit and the licence notice. That is
  what this file is for — it ships with the site, so the credit travels with the
  photos. CC BY-SA additionally means that if you redistribute a modified version
  of the photo, the modification carries the same licence. Resizing and
  re-encoding is a modification in the ordinary sense; keeping this file intact
  satisfies the attribution either way.
- If CampusLend ever becomes commercial, these licences still permit commercial
  use. CC BY-SA does not infect the rest of your site — it applies to the photos,
  not to the software that displays them.

## Replacing them with your own

The honest long-term answer is photographs of the actual items on your campus.
They will look better than any stock photo, because they are the real thing. Drop
them in `assets/img/items/` with the same filenames and delete this file's
corresponding rows.
